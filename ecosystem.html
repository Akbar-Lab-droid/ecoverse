The Living System

Four pillars keep Earth alive

Everything is connected. A forest in one place shapes rain in another; the ocean feeds the air we breathe. Hover a card — it tilts in 3D.

Forest

Trees are the planet's lungs. They absorb CO₂, hold soil together, shelter 80% of land species and seed the rain.

~3 trillion trees on Earth

Ocean

The ocean covers 71% of the planet, stores most of its heat and carbon, and feeds billions of people.

71% of Earth's surface

Clean Air

Plankton and forests produce about half of the oxygen we breathe. Clean air is a shared, fragile commons.

~50% of oxygen from the ocean

Renewable Energy

Sun, wind and water deliver thousands of times more energy than humanity uses — clean, silent and endless.

~30% of global electricity
