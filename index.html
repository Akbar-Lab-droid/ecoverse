The Living System

Four pillars keep Earth alive

Everything is connected. A forest in one place shapes rain in another; the ocean feeds the air we breathe. Hover a card — it tilts in 3D.

Forest

Trees are the planet's lungs. They absorb CO₂, hold soil together, shelter 80% of land species and seed the rain.

~3 trillion trees on Earth

Ocean

The ocean covers 71% of the planet, stores most of its heat and carbon, and feeds billions of people.

71% of Earth's surface

Clean Air

Plankton and forests produce about half of the oxygen we breathe. Clean air is a shared, fragile commons.

~50% of oxygen from the ocean

Renewable Energy

Sun, wind and water deliver thousands of times more energy than humanity uses — clean, silent and endless.

~30% of global electricity

Small Steps, Big System

Six habits that heal the planet

No one has to do everything. Everyone can do one thing — and systems change when millions do.

01

Carry a reusable bottle

One bottle replaces ~167 plastic bottles a year and the oil used to make them.

Saves ~7 kg plastic / year
02

Plant a tree — or fund one

A single tree absorbs about 21 kg of CO₂ every year for decades.

~21 kg CO₂ / tree / year
03

Switch to LED + solar

LEDs use up to 80% less energy than old bulbs; rooftop solar pays back in a few years.

Up to 80% less lighting energy
04

Walk, bike, share rides

Short car trips are the dirtiest per kilometre. Active travel is free exercise too.

~0.12 kg CO₂ saved / km
05

Eat one more plant meal a week

Plant-based meals usually carry a far smaller footprint than meat-heavy ones.

~0.5 t CO₂ / year if fully plant-based
06

Recycle & repair

Recycling aluminium saves 95% of the energy of making it new; repair beats replace.

95% energy saved on aluminium
